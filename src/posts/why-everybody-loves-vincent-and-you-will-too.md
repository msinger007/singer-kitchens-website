<div class="post-body"><p>At Singer Kitchens, everybody loves Vincent!</p>
<p>One of our most beloved designers at Singer Kitchens, Vincent uses his creativity and attention to detail to help our clients fall in love with their kitchens all over again.</p>
<p>Vincent’s kitchen designs are the talk of the town, everybody loves them, and him! Whether you're looking for a traditional or contemporary kitchen design, Vincent will work with you to create a beautiful and functional space.</p>
<p>Vincent’s designs have been featured in various publications, such as <em>New Orleans Magazine, House Beautiful</em>, and within the pages of <em>Inside New Orleans</em>. As well as the HGTV shows <em>Bang for Your Buck</em> and <em>New Orleans Reno</em>.</p>
<p>There's nobody quite like Vincent when it comes to design. Let us show you why everybody loves him!<br>
<h2>Why Do Our Clients Love Vincent?</h2><br>
Vincent’s personalized attention and investment in our clients’ projects are clear from the start. He always makes it a point to understand his client’s visions and what they are trying to achieve.</p>
<p><strong><em>“I can design it and give them an accurate estimate,”</em></strong> Vincent said.<strong><em> “If they agree, we go out to measure and the process begins.”</em></strong></p>
<p>The ideas and process are always a hit with customers, who love how he takes their visions and brings them to life. From small ideas to inspiration from pictures on Pinterest, Vincent makes sure to create a custom solution that fits their vision perfectly, <a href="/a-downsized-dream-kitchen-in-metairie/">as seen in Maggie and Roy's Metairie Condo project.</a></p>
<p>As a local native, Vincent also has an intimate knowledge of New Orleans and is always able to navigate through it with ease. This knowledge of New Orleans gives him a chance to familiarize himself with the neighborhoods that our clients live in to help him with designs and ideas, <a href="/preserving-a-rich-history-in-a-new-orleans-kitchen/">like with Sherree's kitchen</a>.</p>
<p>Connecting with people also plays a huge factor; not only does Vincent know how to make friends quickly, but he also keeps them by knowing exactly what they need from him at any given time!</p>
<p><strong><em>“I’ve worked in retail in the past, and it’s really helped me develop customer relationships,” </em></strong>Vincent said.</p>
<p>With over half of his clients being referrals, it's not hard to see why people keep coming back for more! Price is not much of a concern when the clients know that Vincent is taking care of everything!</p>
<p><strong><em>“We [Singer Kitchens] have the same price points as Home Depot with a lot more love,” </em></strong>Vincent said.<strong><em> “The problem with the big box stores is that the person in that department isn’t a specialist. That’s what we do.” </em></strong><br>
<h2>What About His Time with Singer Kitchens?</h2><br>
Vincent enjoys his quality of life with us as his employer. At 51, he started on a new career journey with us, and he fell in love with it.</p>
<p>He provides a sense of joy and enthusiasm that's infectious and gets people to come back to us or to start a new project with us.</p>
<p>With us, he can also work from home to take more time to work with the SingerSight™ computer-aided design (CAD) software and even takes care of designs overnight. He’s able to make sure our customers are involved in the creative process to flesh out the designs and ideas.</p>
<p>“We work with everyone from age 25 to 80 and have had great experiences across the board,” Vincent said.</p>
<p>Also, leave it to Singer to keep things updated with our customers!</p>
<p>From coordination of timelines to delivery of materials, we handle it all. Vincent helps us go that extra mile to ensure that those timelines are met!<br>
<h3>Lagniappe: Vincent is a Dog Lover/Rescuer!</h3><br>
When we say everybody loves Vincent, we mean everybody including our four-legged friends!</p>
<p>Vincent is a beacon for lost or homeless dogs and has been finding owners or adopting lost dogs since Hurricane Katrina. In fact, on more than one occasion, lost dogs have found <em>Vincent</em> while he’s estimating or designing for a Singer Kitchens client.</p>
<p>When our Carrollton location was in the final steps of being completed,  we had the doors open to let the epoxy fumigate. And here comes Freckles walking right through our door, right up to Vincent. The rest is history.</p>
<p>And when a client came in asking for a cabinet color to match their beautiful standard Poodle, Colette, we knew just who to call.</p>
<p>Vincent has a heart of gold.<br>
