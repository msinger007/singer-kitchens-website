<div class="post-body"><div class="post-section"><div class="el-text"><p>Do you ever have a vision or dream about something you really want?</p>
<p>You can see what it looks like, but the details are blurry.</p>
<p>If you wanted to go out &amp; buy that thing, you might have a hard time finding it.</p>
<p>Our client Don had this exact problem.</p>
<p>He had a vision of what he wanted his kitchen to look like. He found bits and pieces of things he liked here &amp; there, but the full picture escaped him.</p>
<p>Well, it did. Until he visited our showroom.</p>
<h2>You’ll Know It When You See It</h2>
Don was admiring one of our showroom displays featuring a New Orleans-style kitchen complete with inset cabinets &amp; fleur de lis hardware.
<p>That’s when we overheard him exclaim <em>“that’s it… that’s exactly what I want”</em> from around the corner.</p>
<p>It’s the kind of thing that only happens in a showroom.</p>
<p>The kitchen display Don saw in our showroom was NOT actually what he wanted in his house. It was the elements of the kitchen he was in love with:<br>
<ul><br>
<li>New Orleans theme</li><br>
<li>Inset cabinets</li><br>
<li>Color scheme</li><br>
<li>Clean hardware</li><br>
</ul><br>
That’s when our team got involved.</p>
<h2>Ironing Out the Foggy Details</h2>
We visited Don’s home to get measurements &amp; see what his home was like. He lived near a marina &amp; had an amazing view of the water, so we wanted to make sure his new kitchen gave him the best seat in the house.
<p>Then, we created a Pinterest board to share pictures &amp; ideas of the kitchen setup we thought he would like. Pinterest has turned out to be a super handy tool for planning a kitchen!</p>
<p>One of the hardest things for Don to settle on was his choice of countertops. So, we did what usually works best - we took him to our favorite dealer &amp; walked the yard until it happened.</p>
<p><em>“There, that piece,”</em> he said. It was love at first sight.</p>
<p>When you see things in person, the perfect item will usually find you!</p>
<h2>Time to Renovate</h2>
Once we had the selections done, it was time to begin the renovation. Our team tore out the old kitchen and installed the new one.
<p>We even brought in a crane to handle the extraordinary piece of granite he had picked out for his kitchen island.</p>

<h2>Another Happy Ending</h2>
Don loves his new kitchen so much, he’s always entertaining company. He tells everyone all about how his dream became a reality.
<p>And, every morning when he drinks his coffee in his brand new kitchen, he gets to look out across his deck at the water.</p>
<p>Don got the kitchen of his dreams. Literally.</p>
