<h2>How to Spot Water Damage in Your Kitchen</h2><br>
While mold is a more obvious symptom of water damage, there are a few that you may be surprised at. Are you seeing any of these tell tale signs in your kitchen?<br>
<ul><br>
<li>Cabinets are no longer closing or sitting properly</li><br>
<li>Warped or discolored cabinets or flooring</li><br>
<li>Bubbling paint or flooring</li><br>
<li>Mold</li><br>
</ul><br>
