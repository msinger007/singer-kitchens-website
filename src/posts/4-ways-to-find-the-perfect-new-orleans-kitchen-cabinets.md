<div class="post-body"><p>The seasons are changing, the holidays are fast approaching, and Hurricane Ida still looms in our recent memories. Either out of holiday inspiration or storm-inflicted necessity, you may find yourself suddenly in search of a new kitchen. Why not take this chance to craft your DREAM kitchen?</p>
<p>Singer Kitchens is here with all the tools you need to make the process simple, stress-free, and fun!</p>
<p>Planning your new kitchen should be exciting, not overwhelming. With our 90 years of experience turning our clients’ visions into reality, we’ve narrowed down a few simple questions to help guide you through the design process. Ready to start planning your perfect kitchen?</p>
<p>Here are 4 ways to get started with the process from our Singer Design Specialists.<br>
<h2>1. What is your style?</h2><br>
Your kitchen is a perfect opportunity to reflect your personal aesthetic and lifestyle. It should be cohesive with the look of your home, and flow seamlessly into your other rooms.</p>
<p>If you’re not sure how to define your home’s aesthetic, look around your house and pinpoint what brings you joy and comfort in your home. Collect inspiration photos online or photograph the elements of your home decor that you love and share these with one of our design specialists.<br>
<h2>2.  What is the most important thing to you?</h2><br>
There are four main ingredients that work together in every kitchen.<br>
<ul><br>
<li>Design (look)</li><br>
<li>Cost,</li><br>
<li>Construction</li><br>
<li>Function</li><br>
</ul><br>
Everyone prioritizes these elements differently, and there is no right formula, just a right formula for you! Think about how you would prioritize these items, and be sure to convey those needs to your designer.<br>
<h2>3. What is your budget?</h2><br>
Having a clear picture of your budget for your project is crucial for creating the ideal kitchen for you. Not only will it help your designer recommend the best layout and door style for you, we’ll be able to maximize the functionality of your kitchen by helping you prioritize your accessories and add-ons within your budget.</p>
<p>Don’t forget to speak with our specialists about our financing options, many of which have interest-free packages and can be a great way to help you attain your dream kitchen. <u><a href="/showrooms/">Contact us today for more information.</a></u><br>
<h2>4.  How do you use your kitchen?</h2><br>
A well designed kitchen is one that supports your lifestyle. Do you like to entertain? Do you bake frequently? Does the family like to cook together? Your kitchen layout should reflect how you use it, not dictate how you use it.</p>
<p>Storage is also a key element to kitchen usage. Do you like your pots within easy reach of the stove, or tucked away? Do you have seasonal dinnerware you’d like to hide away for most of the year? Unique storage solutions are endless with our large selection of accessories and different layout options.</p>
<p>Be sure to communicate how you use your kitchen so our specialists can create an expertly customized kitchen specific to your needs.<br>
