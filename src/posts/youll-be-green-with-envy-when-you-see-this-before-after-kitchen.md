<div class="post-body"><div class="post-section"><div class="el-text"><p>People are obsessed with this Green Kitchen renovation that we just completed. More importantly, so is our client!</p><p>At first, she wasn’t so sure about the green. In fact, she told us choosing a bold color felt a little scary.</p><p>And it wasn’t just the color decision that scared her, it was making decisions for the entire renovation. From changing out the cabinets to choosing a new layout to solving old storage problems, she wanted to be confident that her new kitchen would be done right.</p><p>Our client confided in us: “If I were to do this on my own, all those decisions would have overwhelmed me.
