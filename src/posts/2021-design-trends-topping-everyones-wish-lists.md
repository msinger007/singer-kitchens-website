<div class="post-body"><p>Over the past year, we’ve all been spending more time in the place we love the most, home.</p>
<p>We’ve also been updating our homes like never before.</p>
<p>In fact, in late summer 2020, Americans tipped off <a href="https://www.npr.org/2020/09/11/909264580/why-home-improvement-has-surged-and-how-its-changing-america" target="_blank" rel="noopener">a surge of home improvement spending</a>, at the height of quarantine. And this new drive toward renovation hasn’t shown signs of slowing down yet.</p>
<p>To say the least, we’re excited and keeping our eyes on new design trends like never before!</p>
<p>Our designers have worked closely with many Northshore &amp; Southshore clients over the past year to conceptualize, design, and bring to life <a href="/how-to-get-your-dream-kitchen-even-if-the-vision-is-a-little-blurry-in-your-head/"><em>dream worthy</em> kitchens</a>, bathrooms, and <a href="/closets/">closets</a>.</p>
<p>You can see some of them in our project <a href="/portfolio/">gallery</a>.</p>
<p>We’ve seen new trends emerge and classic timeless trends re-emerge.</p>
<p>Here’s what we’re seeing in 2021.</p>
<h2 id="h-smart-storage-cabinetry-takes-priority">Smart Storage Cabinetry Takes Priority</h2>
<div>
<figure><img src="/images/2021/04/041921b.webp" alt="2021 Design Trends Topping EVERYONE’S Wish Lists!" width="1080" height="1080" fetchpriority="high"></figure>
</div>
<p>We’ve sung the praises of <a href="/21-storage-solutions-to-start-your-2021-off-right/">inventive kitchen storage solutions</a> before, and this year, they’ve really taken off!</p>
<p>Some of our client favorites include deep kitchen drawers, the always-functional classic lazy susan, built-in home ‘command centers,’ tiered spice cabinets, utensil drawers, roll-out pantry trays, designated pet supply storage, and more.</p>
<p>These solutions will help tame your beloved things while keeping them easily accessible for daily use.</p>
<h2 id="h-soaring-kitchen-islands-are-still-a-must-have">Soaring Kitchen Islands are Still a Must-Have</h2>
<div>
<figure><img src="/images/2021/04/041921c.webp" alt="2021 Design Trends Topping EVERYONE’S Wish Lists!" width="1080" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
</div>
<p>If there’s one kitchen trend that is consistently wished for and has been for decades, it’s the <a href="/what-is-a-kitchen-island-and-why-you-need-one-a-designers-guide/">kitchen island</a>.</p>
<p>Sure, textures and materials have changed over the years – quartz and marble are winning over granite as of late – but the idea has remained the same. One smooth &amp; pristine catchall area that’s multipurpose.</p>
<p>It can handle cooking prep, daily meals, homework, work tasks, laundry folding, and even gift-wrapping, <a href="/a-downsized-dream-kitchen-in-metairie/">as one of our recent clients told us</a>.</p>
<h2 id="h-multipurpose-rooms-on-the-rise">Multipurpose Rooms on the Rise</h2>
<p>Speaking of multi-purpose, it’s no surprise that multi-functional rooms have become necessities. Our kids may be learning virtually, we might be working remotely, and we’re enjoying extracurricular activities we might have enjoyed elsewhere prior to 2020.</p>
<div>
<figure><img src="/images/2021/04/041921d.webp" alt="2021 Design Trends Topping EVERYONE’S Wish Lists!" width="1080" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
</div>
<figure><img src="/images/2021/04/041921e.webp" alt="2021 Design Trends Topping EVERYONE’S Wish Lists!" width="1080" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
<p>One way to create a great multipurpose space is by leveraging the power of the classic <a href="/closets/">murphy bed</a>. Also called wall beds, murphy beds make for an easy transformation of a home office, a homeschool room, a craft room, or a workout studio when you’re ready to welcome your guests.</p>
<h2 id="h-cool-muted-shades-earthy-tones-still-reign-in-color">Cool Muted Shades &amp; Earthy Tones Still Reign in Color</h2>
<div>
<figure><img src="/images/2021/04/041921f.webp" alt="2021 Design Trends Topping EVERYONE’S Wish Lists!" width="600" height="600" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
</div>
<p>When it comes to color, we’re seeing warm white shades, cool grays (the <a href="https://www.pantone.com/color-of-the-year-2021" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Pantone color of 2021</a>!), and earthy tones still outshining the rest.</p>
<p>Our clients love to mix cool neutrals for walls, cabinets, and countertops – and add elements of mixed metal hardware and natural textures into their updated spaces.</p>
