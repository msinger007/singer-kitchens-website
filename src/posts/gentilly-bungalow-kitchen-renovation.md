<div class="post-body"><p>When Bev decided to downsize from her large Uptown home, she was ready for a new chapter filled with comfort, history and charm. She and her best childhood friend purchased a double bungalow in Gentilly and began transforming it into a place that reflected their new stage in life. But once Bev settled in, she quickly discovered that downsizing came with one big challenge: less storage.</p>
<p><strong>A Kitchen That Needed Care</strong></p>
<p>The kitchen, in particular, didn’t feel like home. The existing cabinets were cheaply made, falling apart and didn’t match Bev’s taste or needs.</p>
<p>This would also be a milestone project for Bev. It was her first kitchen renovation done entirely on her own. In the past, her husband had always taken the lead. This time, she would have to make every decision herself and create a kitchen that truly reflected her style.</p>

<p><strong>Finding the Right Fit</strong></p>
<p>A friend suggested she visit Singer Kitchens, and it turned out to be the perfect advice. As Bev explored the Mid-City showroom, the full-size displays and thoughtfully curated finishes helped her visualize how her future kitchen could look and function.</p>
<p>Bev already knew she loved the classic pairing of navy blue and white cabinets and the timeless shaker door style. When she met Vincent, her designer at Singer Kitchens, everything began to take shape. With more than twenty years of design experience, Vincent immediately understood how to help Bev achieve both beauty and functionality in her smaller space.</p>
<p><strong>Thoughtful Design, Tailored to Her</strong></p>
<p>Vincent suggested replacing a cluttered bookcase near the narrow walkway with a reduced-depth pantry cabinet. He then connected the pantry to the main cabinetry using an angled transition cabinet, which created a smoother flow and added much-needed storage.</p>
<p>Bev had always loved having her pots and pans hanging on a pegboard in her previous kitchen, but in this new layout, the only available wall was right by the back door. Vincent designed a custom-depth enclosure that allowed her to keep her pegboard setup hidden behind cabinet doors, preserving her favorite feature while keeping the design clean and polished.</p>
<p>He also included two and three-drawer base cabinets for efficient organization and a hidden wastebasket cabinet to make daily life easier. These thoughtful details made Bev’s kitchen both practical and personal.</p>

<p><strong>A Collaboration That Clicked</strong></p>
<p>Bev found Vincent easy to work with and appreciated how he guided her through each step with patience and care. “Vincent was extremely helpful and made me feel comfortable,” she shared. His professionalism and creativity gave her confidence that every choice was the right one.</p>
<p><strong>A Kitchen That Reflects Her</strong></p>
<p>When the renovation was complete, Bev was thrilled with the result. “When they finished it, I thought I have never been so happy with a finished project,” she said. “I just smile every day when I walk into my beautiful kitchen.”</p>
<p>Her new space blends classic design with smart function. The navy and white color palette, Slim Trim shaker doors, and quartz countertops come together to create a look that is both fresh and timeless. Most importantly, it feels like Bev herself, a reflection of her taste, her independence, and her love of her new home.</p>
