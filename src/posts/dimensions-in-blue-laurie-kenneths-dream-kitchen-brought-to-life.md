<div class="post-body"><p>For Laurie and Kenneth, blue has always been more than just a favorite color. For them, blue brings a sense of calm and sophistication. When they set out to update their kitchen, they wanted the space to reflect their tastes while matching the fresh, modern feel of the rest of their home. They had previously tried to achieve this by painting their existing cabinets. But the paint quickly began to peel, leaving them frustrated and looking for a more durable solution. That’s when they decided it was time to create the kitchen they had dreamed of.</p>
<p><strong>Building on a Vision</strong></p>
<p>Laurie and Kenneth had a clear idea of what they wanted. They were ready to invest in a space that felt beautiful, functional, and personal. To help bring that vision to life, they turned to Singer Kitchens and worked closely with designer Holly, who asked thoughtful questions to fully understand their goals and then helped guide them through each step in the process.</p>
<p>They chose factory painted cabinetry in a soft, nature-inspired blue called Maritime. The color of the cabinets not only brought the soothing tone they loved into the heart of their home but also provided the durability and quality they had missed with their previous paint job. Factory finishes are cured under controlled conditions, which makes them more resistant to wear and better suited for everyday use.</p>

<p><strong>Designed for Their Lifestyle</strong></p>
<p>Laurie and Kenneth are both tall, and their kitchen needed to work for them. Holly helped adjust the wall cabinet height for better comfort and functionality while also creating space for their taller countertop appliances.</p>
<p>They also wanted to improve organization. With Holly’s guidance, they added multiple drawer stacks that gave them more usable storage and a more efficient layout. Slim shaker cabinet doors offered a clean, timeless look that felt right at home in the newly updated space.</p>
<p><strong>Trust Built Over Generations</strong></p>
<p>For Laurie and Kenneth, choosing Singer Kitchens was an easy decision.</p>
<p>“We knew the trusted name of Singer Kitchens quite well. Both of our grandparents used Singer, and our aunts and uncles too.”</p>
<p>From their first meeting, Holly made them feel heard and supported.</p>
<p>“Holly just fit with us. She understood our vision and made us feel comfortable. She stayed on top of every detail, and her installers knew and understood her standards.”  Laurie appreciated the continuous support Holly provided throughout the process, “She was always there to answer a phone call or a text message to make sure everything was going smoothly.”</p>

<p><strong>A Kitchen That Feels Like Home</strong></p>
<p>Every element of their new kitchen reflects Laurie and Kenneth’s shared vision. It’s a space where they enjoy cooking, gathering, and simply spending time together.</p>
<p>“Every day I wake up and I can’t believe this is my kitchen.”<br>
“My husband and I each had our own vision of our new kitchen, and Holly helped us achieve the kitchen that was perfect for both of us.”</p>
<p>Their new kitchen isn’t just a renovation, it’s a reflection of who they are and how they live. It combines thoughtful design, trusted craftsmanship, and personal touches that make the space truly their own.</p>
<p>“Everyone and anyone I know that even thinks about renovating their kitchen, I’m sending them to Singer.”</p></div>
